Privacy policy

Provisional version, under review. Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who we are

Mozambique Camp Adventures (“Moz Camp Adventures”), Marina, Escola Náutica, Maputo City, Mozambique. Contact: info@mozcampadventures.co.mz · +258 84 440 6543.

What we collect and why

  • Booking and contact requests: name, phone, e-mail, dates, number of participants and your message. They are stored on our own server and used only to reply and arrange your experience. We also keep an irreversible fingerprint (hash) of your IP address to protect against abuse.
  • Live Chat: name, e-mail, phone (optional), subject, page of origin, and the messages and files you send. They are stored on our own server for 12 months after the last message and used only to reply. If you leave the website before we reply, we send the reply by e-mail.
  • WhatsApp messages: only if you agree when booking, we use your number to send updates about that booking through Meta WhatsApp Business. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.
  • Reviews: name, origin (optional), rating and text. Stored on our own server and published only after our team approves them.
  • Customer account (optional): name, e-mail, phone (optional), language and password (stored encrypted, never as plain text). It lets you see your bookings and documents. Bookings you make while signed in are linked to the account. You can delete the account at any time under “My account”; bookings and tax documents already issued are kept, as required by law.
  • Promotions: only if you explicitly agree, when creating the account or under “My account”. We record the date of your consent. You can stop at any time under “My account”.
  • Usage statistics: only with your consent, through Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager), as aggregated data.

Cookies

We use essential local storage to remember your cookie preferences and, if you sign in to your account or start a Live Chat conversation, an essential session cookie. Analytics cookies are only enabled if you accept them. You can change your choice under “Manage cookies” in the footer.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We only share what is needed with the providers involved in your experience (for example the accommodation booked) and the technical services mentioned above.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data at info@mozcampadventures.co.mz.
