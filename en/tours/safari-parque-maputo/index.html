Overview

Maputo Special Reserve (formerly the Elephant Reserve) is an elephant paradise — with over 700 individuals roaming free. But it is not just elephants: hippos, lions, zebras, flamingos and over 300 bird species inhabit this natural paradise just 1 hour from Maputo.

Our day trip includes 4x4 transport, a guided game drive, a visit to the reserve's coastal area with pink flamingos and a picnic by the lagoon.

An accessible and unforgettable safari, perfect for families and those with limited days in Maputo.
