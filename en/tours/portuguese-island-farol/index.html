Portuguese Island & Lighthouse Beach

A private journey through Maputo Bay

Ilha dos Portugueses, Inhaca & Praia do FarolFull day (~10h)

Overview

This experience focuses on exploring the natural beauty and culture of Inhaca Island.

Guests enjoy pristine beaches, a traditional island lunch, and a guided safari around the island.

It is ideal for travelers looking for a more relaxed and cultural island experience.

DurationFull day (~10h)
LocationIlha dos Portugueses, Inhaca & Praia do Farol
Destinations3 destinos
Minimum5 people
ChildrenChildren (4–10 years) · 5.000 MZN
CancellationAsk our team

Highlights

  • Private tour — exclusive boat for your group
  • Stop at Portuguese Island
  • Authentic lunch in Inhaca Village
  • Guided island safari on Inhaca Island
  • Afternoon at the remote and stunning Lighthouse Beach

Itinerary

  1. 07:30

    Departure from Maputo Marina

    Board at Maputo Marina. The private boat departs towards Portuguese Island on a morning cruise across Maputo Bay.

  2. 09:00

    Portuguese Island

    Arrival at Portuguese Island. Free time to explore the island, swim in crystal-clear waters and enjoy untouched nature.

  3. 11:30

    Lunch in Inhaca Village

    Lunch at a local restaurant in Inhaca Village with traditional Mozambican dishes prepared with fresh ingredients.

  4. 13:00

    Island Safari

    Guided tour of Inhaca Island: endemic flora, local culture and historic points of interest on the island.

  5. 13:45

    Lighthouse Beach

    Arrival at Lighthouse Beach — one of the most remote and breathtaking beaches in Maputo Bay. Free time to relax and swim.

  6. 15:45

    Return Journey

    Departure from Lighthouse Beach back to Maputo Marina.

  7. 17:30

    Arrival in Maputo

    Arrival at Maputo Marina with unforgettable memories of a remarkable day.
