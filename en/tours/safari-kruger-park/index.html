Overview

Kruger National Park is Africa's largest and most famous safari destination, spanning over 20,000km² of savannah teeming with the Big 5 and 147 other mammal species. Our full-day trip departs Maputo at 05:00, entering through the Crocodile Bridge Gate for morning and afternoon game drives.

Choose your safari vehicle: the closed 4x4 with pop-up roof (recommended for private groups and protection from wind and dust) or the classic open safari vehicle with elevated seats and 360° visibility for photography.

With a professional safari guide, you will explore the park's roads in search of elephants, lions, giraffes, zebras, buffalo, antelopes and hundreds of bird species.

Itinerary

  1. 05:00

    Departure from Maputo

    Pick-up at hotel or meeting point. Transfer towards the Mozambique – South Africa border.

    • Valid passport
    • Warm layers (early morning)
  2. 06:30

    Border Crossing

    Assistance with border formalities. Continue towards Kruger National Park.

  3. 07:00

    Arrival at Kruger National Park

    Entry through Crocodile Bridge Gate. Beginning of the safari experience.

  4. Morning

    Morning Safari Drive

    Explore the park with a professional guide. Possibility to see: elephants, lions, giraffes, zebras, buffalo, antelopes and many bird species.

  5. 12:00

    Rest Stop at Camp

    Stop at a rest camp inside the park. Lunch included, short rest and photos.

  6. Afternoon

    Afternoon Safari

    Continue exploring other areas of the park with wildlife viewing and scenic stops.

  7. 15:30

    Exit the Park

    Begin return journey to Maputo.

  8. 18:30

    Arrival in Maputo

    Arrival in Maputo and end of the excursion.
