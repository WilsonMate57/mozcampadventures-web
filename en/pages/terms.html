Requests and confirmation

Requests made through the website, WhatsApp or e-mail are not confirmed bookings. A booking is confirmed once our team confirms availability and payment is received.

Prices

Published prices are in Mozambican meticais (MZN) and include applicable taxes. The total shown on the website is an estimate based on published rates; the final amount is the one stated in our team’s confirmation.

Payments

We accept M-Pesa, e-Mola and bank transfer. Payment instructions are sent once availability is confirmed.

Minimum and maximum participants

Some experiences require a minimum number of participants or have a maximum capacity, shown on each experience page.

Cancellations and changes

Cancellation terms are shown on each experience page. Where none are shown, the terms communicated by our team in the confirmation apply.

Weather and safety

Sea activities depend on weather conditions and may be rescheduled by our team for safety reasons.
